> i don't think using a language model this way requires an apology. i do think it requires saying so.
— nisarga, [on-llms](https://ni5arga.github.io/on-llms/)

The ideas here are mine, all of them. I mean, why write? Just [because]({% link Content/Why-I-Write.md %}). But I want a Tarantino style to mine.

I don't want the llm generate-and-review loop. There are folks doing that, and it's auto-fellatio for the intellect.

It's something I do too. I formalize something I wrote too casually, or loosen an obscure idea that comes up in my head. I have a tendency to rant illiterate weird examples irl lol.

I hate reading AI slop. But done right, I'll take the machine's sentence over mine, because it can out-phrase me but it can't out-mean me.

Readership is a spectrum. Tharoor, Thoreau on one end, Manson and the substack crowd on the other. I'm not writing to be read. These are things I'd defend with a bat in a bar.
